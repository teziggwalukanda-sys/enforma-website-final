Films for brands that care how they look.

Enforma is run by Lukanda, a Melbourne director and cinematographer.

We make brand films, interview-led series and campaign content, mostly for property and construction, fashion and sport. Most jobs are small crews and one person who sees it through: the same eye frames the shot, cuts the edit and grades the final.

The work sits between a production house and a content team. It's cinematic enough to lead a brand, and planned so there's a steady flow of content after it.

Who we work with
  • Property & construction →Builders, developers and agents. Project films, interview series and homes shot like short films.
  • Fashion →Labels and retailers. Campaign films, behind the scenes and launch content.
  • Sport →Fighters, teams and events. Short documentaries, profiles and fast event coverage.
  • Websites, apps & AI →Beyond film. Shopify stores, custom apps and AI built into the tools you already use.
What we make
  • Brand filmsThe hero piece. One to three minutes about who you are.
  • Interview seriesYour people on camera, cut into a run of episodes.
  • Content retainersA monthly shoot and edits for Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn.
  • Documentary & profilesShort films about a person, told in their own words.
How a job runs
  1. 01Plan

    A short call, then a one-page brief: the story, the shots, the people and where it will live.

  2. 02Shoot

    Usually one day. Cinema cameras, small crew, little disruption to your team.

  3. 03Edit

    First cut in about a week, two rounds of changes, then a graded master and the cutdowns.
