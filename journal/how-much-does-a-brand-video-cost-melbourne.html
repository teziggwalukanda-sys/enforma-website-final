How Much Does a Brand Video Cost in Melbourne?

The honest answer is that it depends. Here is exactly what it depends on, so you can plan a budget that works.

Type
Guide
For
Marketing teams and business owners
Read

Date
October 2026

It's the first question almost every client asks, and it's a fair one. The honest answer is that a brand video can cost very different amounts depending on what it needs to do. Rather than give a number that doesn't fit your project, here is what actually moves the price, so you can plan a budget with your eyes open.

Planning. A good video is mostly decided before the camera comes out. The story, the people on camera, the shot list and where it will be posted. A clear brief keeps the shoot short and the edit simple. A vague one costs more later.

Shoot days. This is the biggest single factor. Most brand films and interview pieces can be shot in one well-planned day. More locations, more people to interview, or footage that has to be captured over weeks, like a build or an event, means more days.

Crew and gear. A small crew keeps costs down and stays out of the way of your team. Bigger productions add lighting, sound, a second camera, a drone or a producer. Each one adds polish and adds cost, so we only bring what the brief needs.

The edit. A single two-minute film is one job. A hero film plus three cutdowns plus vertical versions for social is a bigger one. Changes, music licensing, colour grading and subtitles all sit here too.

Where you get the most value. The smartest budgets plan one shoot to feed many outputs: a hero film for the website, shorter cuts for ads, and a run of social posts. One day on set, months of content.

If you're planning a project, the easiest way to get a real number is to tell us what you're making and roughly what you'd like to spend. We'll tell you straight what that gets you, or how to get the most out of it.
