Property Video vs Photos: What Actually Sells a Home

Photos get the click. Video gets the feeling. Here is how the two work together in a Melbourne campaign.

Type
Guide
For
Agents, advocates and developers
Read
3 minutes
Date
October 2026

Every property campaign has photos. Fewer have a proper video. Both have a job, and they're not the same job.

Photos are the first impression. They're what people scroll past on the listing sites, and a strong hero shot decides whether someone clicks. They're fast to take in and easy to compare.

Video is what makes someone imagine living there. How the light moves through the house, how one room leads to the next, what the street feels like. A photo shows the kitchen. A video shows walking into it on a Saturday morning.

Video also travels further. A short vertical cut works on Instagram and TikTok where listing photos don't, and a longer walkthrough gives interstate and overseas buyers a real sense of the home before they book an inspection.

The best campaigns use both, planned together. One visit, photos for the listing, a cinematic walkthrough for the hero, and short cuts for social. The agent or advocate on camera adds trust that a gallery can't.

That's how we approached our Glen Iris film for buyer's advocate Aife Raveche. Slow interiors, her voice guiding the viewer, nothing competing with the home.
