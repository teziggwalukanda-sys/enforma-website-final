Questions
Can you film on a live construction site?

Yes. We plan the day with your site team, follow your site's safety requirements and inductions, and keep the crew small so work can carry on around us.

Do you shoot property for agents and buyer's advocates?

Yes. Cinematic walkthroughs, agent or advocate pieces to camera, and short vertical cuts for social, usually from a single visit.

How long does a shoot take?

Most interview pieces and property films are shot in one day. Build and project films are filmed over several visits.
