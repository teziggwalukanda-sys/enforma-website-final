Roots Curated Co is a Melbourne store for curly hair and melanated skin, where every brand is Black, Indigenous or Person of Colour-owned. The brief: a store that sells, and that feels like the community behind it.

We started with an audit of the old site, then rebuilt the theme section by section. Each release went up as a private preview first, so the founder could check it before anything changed for customers.

What we built
  • Custom sectionsA new homepage, brand stories for every maker, an About page with their impact, and a FAQ, all built as Shopify sections the team can edit.
  • A cast of charactersOriginal characters that welcome visitors, cheer when something goes in the cart and hang out on the footer.
  • Braid mini-gameA small game on the About page: tap or drag to braid a character's hair, one section at a time.
  • Night modeAfter 9pm on your own clock, the characters swap into satin bonnets and the store suggests one.
  • Smarter cartA free-shipping progress bar and a simple add-on suggestion, right in the cart.
  • Tested before launchAround 90 automated checks on desktop and iPhone before every release, so nothing broke in front of customers.
